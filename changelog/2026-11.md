# SWEET_python Changelog — November 2026

**Highlights:** The advanced DSTs take a site's waste in its close year, as every
other SWEET path has since SWEET#112 (model-output change). Waste sent to an
incinerator stops producing methane on the `am-waste-flow` branch too, as on main
since SWEET#100 (model-output change). The city-level
advanced DST can say why other landfills hold their waste, and how much of their
methane each reason makes. Its mass flow says what each site receives as well as
what it buries, so an incinerator no longer reads as taking no waste.

## Added

- **The city-level advanced DST's mass flow says what each site receives.**
  `run_advanced_dst_city(..., with_mass_flow=True)` adds `sites_received`
  beside `sites`, in the same shape: per landfill in request order, by year.
  `sites` is what a site buries. Since the reject went to 0 (#120), that is
  nothing at a `combusts` facility, so WasteMAP's advanced DST read every
  incinerator as taking no waste: a plan sending all of Bandung's waste to one
  showed it at "100% of disposed · 0 t". `sites_received` is what the city sends
  each site, burned or buried. It equals `sites` at every site that buries its
  waste, and it is what adds up to `landfilled`.
  - Reporting only: no model-output change.
  - Every per-site stream now goes through one helper, `_windowed_pair`, for its
    open/close window and the pre-implement splice, so what a site receives,
    what it deposits and waste from outside the city stay in step.
  - Tests: `tests/test_adst_city_mass_flow.py`,
    `tests/test_adst_city_incineration.py`.

- **The city-level advanced DST says why other landfills hold their waste.**
  `run_advanced_dst_city(..., with_other_landfills_reasons=True)` returns, per
  variant, other landfills' share of each year's waste and their methane, split
  three ways:
  - a listed landfill's share before it opens (`before_opening`);
  - a listed landfill's share after it closes (`after_closing`);
  - what no listed landfill claims (`unassigned`).

  Each reason's methane is its part of every year's deposit rerun through the
  decay kernel, so the three add up to other landfills' own. WasteMAP's advanced
  DST charts both. That shows, for example, that Bandung's other landfills emit
  in 2026 from waste sent before Sarimukti opened in 2006.
  - The share rule moved into `_split_with_other_landfills`, which
    `_with_other_landfills` now builds its request from. Runs are unchanged (no
    model-output change).
  - Tests: `tests/test_adst_city_other_landfills_reasons.py`.
    ([#119](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/119))

## Changed

- **Nothing sent to an incinerator is landfilled unburned, on `am-waste-flow`
  too** (model-output change). SWEET main set `City.combustion_reject_rate` to 0
  in [#100](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/100) (decision
  `combustion-reject`), but the `am-waste-flow` branch, which WasteMAP's advanced
  DST runs on, still had 0.1. A `combusts` facility there buried 10% of what it
  received, unburned and as food, paper and so on, and that 10% decayed. So a
  WasteMAP plan that sent all of Bandung's disposed waste to an incinerator from
  2030 levelled off well above zero instead of falling away. In a test city of
  that size (421,000 t a year to disposal, all of it burnt from 2030), 2050
  methane falls from 1,392 t to 30 t, which is what waste buried before 2030
  still makes. This brings #100 across unchanged: the rate, its decision
  comment, the README line and the tests in
  `tests/test_adst_city_incineration.py`.
  - `tests/test_site_other_source.py`, which exists only on this branch, assumed
    the 10% reject for waste a burning site accepts from outside the city. It
    now holds that the site buries none of it, and, with the reject set, that
    the reject is taken from the site's whole gate as before.
  - `tests/test_combustion_reject_city_paths.py` drives a `City` path end to end:
    a Custom Location in Japan diverts the whole 76% its country default sends to
    combustion, and 90% of it with the reject set to 0.1.
    ([#120](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/120))
  - A `combusts` facility now deposits nothing, so it emits nothing, and the
    combustion pathway on the `City` paths diverts all it takes.
  - The rate is still a setting: a caller can set it on a `City`, and a
    `combusts` facility then deposits that share of its intake, as before.
  - #100's own changelog entry, in October's file on main, stays there; this is
    the entry for the branch.

- **The advanced DSTs take a site's waste in its close year** (model-output
  change). SWEET main made the open and close years both intake years in
  [#112](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/112) (decision
  `intake-through-close-year`), but the `am-waste-flow` branch, which WasteMAP's
  advanced DST runs on, kept the old rule in its own two window functions: a site
  recorded as closing in 2024 took its last waste in 2023, and the city tool sent
  its 2024 share to the other sites. Both now run from the open year through the
  close year: `dst_common.apply_window` (the single-site and city-level advanced
  DSTs) and `advanced_dst_city._accepting_mask` (which sites a city's shares are
  renormalized over). A site that opens and closes in the same year now takes that
  year's waste instead of none.
  - Each closed site gains one year of deposited waste, so its emissions rise from
    its close year on, and in a city the other sites take that much less that year.
    A scenario closure (`siteClosure`) also takes waste in its closure year.
  - `NEVER_CLOSES` stays 2051, so a site with no close year is unchanged.
  - The `city_params` paths on this branch (Climate TRACE's site estimates, the site
    tool and the City DST) still stop a year early; the next merge of main brings
    them #112's rule.
  - Tests: `tests/test_adst_intake_through_close_year.py`; two tests in
    `tests/test_adst_city_other_landfills.py` now pin the new rule.
    ([#118](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/118))
