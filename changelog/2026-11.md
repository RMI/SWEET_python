# SWEET_python Changelog — November 2026

**Highlights:** The city model has a controlled dumpsite. A city's
controlled-dumpsite share, which the model used to drop, now goes to a fourth
disposal bucket with a dump's MCF and a controlled dump's oxidation (no output
change until a caller passes the share).

## Added

- **The city model sends a controlled-dumpsite share to a controlled dumpsite**
  (no output change until a caller passes the share,
  [#121](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/121)). The generic city model had
  three disposal buckets: a landfill with gas capture, one without, and a dumpsite.
  A controlled-dumpsite share, such as the "aterro controlado" share the SINIR
  loader puts in WasteMAP's `city_waste.waste_treatment_controlled_dumpsite_percent`,
  had nowhere to go: `load_andre_params` never read it, and the split was rescaled
  so the other shares filled its place. A city sending 40% to controlled dumps and
  60% to open dumps was modelled as 100% open dump.
  - `SplitFractions` has a `controlled_dumpsite` share, 0 unless given.
    `load_andre_params` reads `waste_treatment_controlled_dumpsite_percent`, and
    `load_csv_new` and `load_from_csv` read `Percent of Waste to Controlled
    Dumpsites (%)`; a row or table without the column sends it nothing. The share
    counts toward the disposal a city reports, so a city whose reported disposal
    adds up to 100% only with it no longer takes its country's default diversion.
  - The city's fourth landfill takes it: the uncategorised MCF (0.6, the same as
    an open dump's) and oxidation 0.05 without gas capture. It is last, at
    `CONTROLLED_DUMPSITE_LANDFILL` (3), so the first three keep their places.
  - The City DST's levers treat it as a site without capture. "Add gas capture"
    gives it a controlled dump's capture (45%) and oxidation with capture (0.10)
    from the year after implementation; it stays a controlled dumpsite, so its MCF
    does not move. "Move waste to existing gas capture" and a new landfill with
    gas capture scale it with the other shares. The new landfill comes after it,
    and a landfill the baseline does not have is the one that takes no waste before
    implementation, where before it was whichever sat at index 3.
  - Checked against `main` for seven cities with no controlled-dumpsite share
    (four cities-table rows, three Custom Locations), baseline and every DST
    lever: identical emissions.
