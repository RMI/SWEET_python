# SWEET_python Changelog — November 2026

**Highlights:** The advanced DSTs take a site's waste in its close year, as every
other SWEET path has since SWEET#112 (model-output change). The city-level
advanced DST can say why other landfills hold their waste, and how much of their
methane each reason makes.

## Added

- **The city-level advanced DST says why other landfills hold their waste.**
  `run_advanced_dst_city(..., with_other_landfills_reasons=True)` returns, per
  variant, other landfills' share of each year's waste and their methane, split
  three ways:
  - a listed landfill's share before it opens (`before_opening`);
  - a listed landfill's share after it closes (`after_closing`);
  - what no listed landfill claims (`unassigned`).

  Each reason's methane is its part of every year's deposit rerun through the
  decay kernel, so the three add up to other landfills' own. WasteMAP's advanced
  DST charts both. That shows, for example, that Bandung's other landfills emit
  in 2026 from waste sent before Sarimukti opened in 2006.
  - The share rule moved into `_split_with_other_landfills`, which
    `_with_other_landfills` now builds its request from. Runs are unchanged (no
    model-output change).
  - Tests: `tests/test_adst_city_other_landfills_reasons.py`.

## Changed

- **The advanced DSTs take a site's waste in its close year** (model-output
  change). SWEET main made the open and close years both intake years in
  [#112](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/112) (decision
  `intake-through-close-year`), but the `am-waste-flow` branch, which WasteMAP's
  advanced DST runs on, kept the old rule in its own two window functions: a site
  recorded as closing in 2024 took its last waste in 2023, and the city tool sent
  its 2024 share to the other sites. Both now run from the open year through the
  close year: `dst_common.apply_window` (the single-site and city-level advanced
  DSTs) and `advanced_dst_city._accepting_mask` (which sites a city's shares are
  renormalized over). A site that opens and closes in the same year now takes that
  year's waste instead of none.
  - Each closed site gains one year of deposited waste, so its emissions rise from
    its close year on, and in a city the other sites take that much less that year.
    A scenario closure (`siteClosure`) also takes waste in its closure year.
  - `NEVER_CLOSES` stays 2051, so a site with no close year is unchanged.
  - The `city_params` paths on this branch (Climate TRACE's site estimates, the site
    tool and the City DST) still stop a year early; the next merge of main brings
    them #112's rule.
  - Tests: `tests/test_adst_intake_through_close_year.py`; two tests in
    `tests/test_adst_city_other_landfills.py` now pin the new rule.
    ([#118](https://github.com/RMI/SWEET_python/pull/118))
